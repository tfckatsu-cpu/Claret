| DelisRedundantEcoHelp | Заявленные возможности | Delis redundant eco help See your idle engineers and your mass extractors' tech level and upgrades at a glance. Idle engineers - Every idle engineer gets a small gold tech marker under its health bar: I for tech 1, II for tech 2, III for tech 3. - Field engineers count as tech 2. Support commanders and your commander get no marker. - The marker disappears as soon as the engineer gets something to do, and comes back when it goes idle again. It follows the same idle list as FAF's own idle engineer button. Mass extractors - Every finished mass extractor shows its tech level under its health bar in green: I, II or III. The different colour keeps them apart from the engineer markers. - An extractor that is upgrading also gets a small gold arrow next to its tech level, for example II with an arrow while a tech 2 extractor upgrades to tech 3. - The arrow disappears when the upgrade is finished or cancelled, and the tech level updates to the new one. Options (optional, needs ReUI) - With the ReUI mod enabled, open ReUI Options from the in-game menu and pick "DelisRedundantEcoHelp". - Switch each marker type on or off: idle engineer tech level, mass extractor tech level, and the arrow on upgrading mass extractors. With both extractor markers off, the mod stops checking your extractors entirely. - Set your own colour for the idle engineer markers, the mass extractor markers and the upgrade arrow with the colour sliders. Markers change colour live while you drag; Save keeps your colours for future games, Cancel puts them back. - Without ReUI the mod works the same with the default colours: gold engineers, green extractors, gold arrow. Good to know - Markers hide together with the rest of the game UI. - Mass extractors are checked about once per second, so a change can show up to a second late. - UI mod: only shows information about your own units, does not touch the simulation, and does not need to be enabled by the host. | Требует проверки по реализации; бинарные части см. compiled-lua.txt | sources/MainMods/DelisRedundantEcoHelp/mod_info.lua; v3 |
